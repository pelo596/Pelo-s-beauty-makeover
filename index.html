The signature look

Soft, sunlit skin

5 steps · 15 minutes · Beginner friendly

Press a hydrating serum into clean skin, smooth on moisturizer, then give it a minute to sink in. Finish with SPF 30 or higher for an easy, radiant base.

Maybelline option: Fit Me Dewy + Smooth Primer. Use your usual separate sunscreen underneath.

Test two shades along your jaw in daylight. Choose the one that disappears into both face and neck, then start at the center and blend outward with a damp sponge.

Maybelline options: Fit Me Dewy + Smooth for a more radiant finish, or Fit Me Matte + Poreless for a matte finish. Shade availability varies.

Tap cream bronzer where the sun naturally hits—temples, upper cheekbones, and a touch across the nose. Blend up and back for a lifted, diffused finish.

Maybelline option: Sunkisser blush for a warm, sun-kissed flush.

Sweep a warm neutral through the crease with a fluffy brush. Press champagne shimmer onto the lid with a fingertip and finish with mascara.

Maybelline options: The Nudes Eyeshadow Palette and Lash Sensational Sky High mascara.

Dust a whisper of powder only where you crease, sweep on cream blush, then mist setting spray from arm's length. Your skin should still look like skin.

Maybelline option: Lifter Gloss for a comfortable, glossy lip to finish your look.

Maybelline product suggestions are examples, not a sponsorship or live stock check. Products, shades, and availability vary by region; choose what suits your skin and check current product details with the retailer.

Your crown, your rules

The Crown & Melt

A patient, beginner-friendly frontal masterclass—from lace prep to that seamless, skin-like finish. Good hair days, meet carry-on life.

Prep & customize: tint the lace, gently pluck, and test your fit before install.

Apply with intention: patch-test adhesive, work in thin layers, and let each layer turn tacky.

Finish the melt: secure with a wrap, style your part, and keep a gentle remover handy.

A little care goes a long way

Use lace-safe remover and follow its directions—never pull at the lace. Clean adhesive gently, let the unit dry fully, detangle from ends upward, and store it away from direct sun. Take a break from tension if your scalp feels irritated.
